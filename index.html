Start with your passport and purpose

Your passport nationality, country of residence, trip purpose and intended stay determine which route to check. The language you read does not determine visa eligibility. Check the responsible embassy or government portal before buying non-refundable travel. Tourism, business, employment, study, family visits and long stays can require different permissions; do not assume a tourist permission allows work.

Prepare the evidence for your route

Use the checklist issued for your exact route. Prepare a passport meeting that route’s validity rules, the requested photograph, itinerary, accommodation details and any onward-travel, financial, invitation or purpose evidence the official service asks for. Names and passport details should match across documents. Check scan quality and file requirements before uploading directly to the government service. This is a preparation list, not a complete official document list for every nationality.

Apply through the responsible official service

Open the verified government portal or contact the consular post responsible for your residence. Confirm that online filing is available for your passport and visa category. Create your own official account, review the application, upload the required evidence and pay any applicable government fee there. Save the submission reference and receipt. Respond to document requests through the official channel; an application receipt is not a visa approval.

Arrival registration is a separate task

The Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC) is separate from a visa. Use the official free TDAC service, check its current submission window and prepare travel and accommodation information. Completing an arrival card does not replace a required visa.

Keep government fees separate from service charges

Our guidance is free; government visa, extension and related processing fees may still apply. Check the official fee schedule and the total at payment, including any disclosed payment-provider charge. Avoid unrequested express services and third-party arrival-form charges. Keep an itemised official receipt. Neither a lower fee nor approval is guaranteed by using this guide.

Track what is actually confirmed

Keep the official application reference privately and check the government account, email or official status service. The board below records only the stage you select yourself. It is not linked to government systems and does not verify status. Do not put passport numbers, photographs, financial evidence or application references into it. The browser-local board is optional; official records remain authoritative.

Plan extensions before your stay expires

An extension of permitted stay is different from obtaining a new visa. Before the date on your latest permission to stay, ask Thai Immigration which application, documents, attendance requirements and official fee apply to your entry category. Do not assume an extension is available or that a pending request permits an overstay.

Your travel timeline

Use these relative milestones as a planning sequence, not a promised processing schedule. Check the authority’s current processing guidance and your own permitted-stay expiry.

  1. Before booking: check nationality, purpose and current entry rules.
  2. Before departure: prepare evidence and allow the official processing period.
  3. After submission: keep the receipt and check official requests and decisions.
  4. Before arrival and stay expiry: complete arrival registration and check any permitted extension.
